## J&K state nodal institution for HSC 2026 and state-level results

### Takeaway
Not found. Official pages describe a state/UT round run by "State Nodal Institutions" (3 winners per State/UT) but do not name the J&K institution or publish J&K state results.

### Cited Findings
- Structure: register on MY Bharat, then a State/UT-level hackathon run by nodal institutions (online pitch, jury, 3 winners per State/UT), then IIT Bombay screening (one team per State/UT, up to 36 finalists), then the national showcase with top 5 prizes. — [MY Bharat HSC page](https://mybharat.gov.in/pages/hack-for-social-cause)
- 2026 cycle timeline: registration opened 27 Oct 2025, submissions due 9 Dec 2025, state-level hackathon 15–17 Dec 2025. — [MY Bharat VBYLD 2026](https://mybharat.gov.in/pages/vbyld_2026)
- The J&K contingent was led by Yasha Mudgal (Commissioner/Secretary, Youth Services and Sports) and Anuradha Gupta (DG Youth Services and Sports). Other J&K VBYLD recognitions in the same release: Sheikh Anzar Amin (PM Gallery, automatic gas regulator for LPG heaters); Suvidha, Kum Kum, Mridul and Nitish selected for the final PPT before the PM. These are VBYLD tracks, not necessarily HSC. — [The INS](https://theins.in/2026/01/14/lg-interacts-with-youth-who-attended-national-youth-festival/)
- The current 2027 cycle (relevant to YatraKavach) is open: registration/submission to 27 Oct 2026 (FAQ says 15 Oct 2026; the page is inconsistent), state-level hackathons 7 Nov – 5 Dec 2026, national showcase 10–12 Jan 2027. — [MY Bharat HSC page](https://mybharat.gov.in/pages/hack-for-social-cause)

### Inferences
- Because IIT Bombay picks only one team per State/UT, A.N.T.S was very likely the J&K state winner for 2026. The other two J&K state winners are unknown. (inference)
- YatraKavach would compete in the J&K state round against other J&K teams. A second disaster-themed J&K entry after A.N.T.S will draw comparison, so clear differentiation matters. (inference)

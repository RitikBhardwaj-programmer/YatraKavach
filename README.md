# YatraKavach

**Route-segment hazard and crowd risk for pilgrim tracks in Jammu & Kashmir, with officer-approved,
multilingual alerts and a tamper-evident audit log.**

Prototype for Hack for Social Cause 2027 (VBYLD 2027, MoYAS, knowledge partner IIT Bombay).
Theme 11: Disaster Mitigation & Resilience. Team kavach (J&K).

> "The warning existed. The decision did not."
> On 26 Aug 2025 a landslide on the old Vaishno Devi track near Adhkuwari killed about 34 pilgrims.
> Archived model rainfall had passed the landslide watch threshold before 06:00 that day
> (see [docs/replay-findings.md](docs/replay-findings.md)).

Status: work in progress. Setup, architecture and limitations sections are added as the build lands.

# Previous Hack for Social Cause (HSC) editions: finalists, winners and what won

Research date: 2026-10-23. Researcher downloaded and read both official PDFs linked from https://mybharat.gov.in/pages/vbyld_2026 (direct CDN links below).

## Q1. Who were the HSC 2026 national winners (1st–5th) and the national finalists?

### Takeaway
The official PDFs exist and were read. The 2026 edition had **24 national finalist teams** (not 36), and the **J&K team "Aether" from IUST Pulwama placed 3rd nationally** with a disaster-resilience project called **A.N.T.S (Disaster Resilient System)**. Two of the top 5 winners were **solo entrants** (1st and 2nd place).

## Q4. Rules and format of HSC 2026 vs HSC 2027 (context for the bar)

### Takeaway
2026 required a working prototype with public repo and demo video; 2027 keeps this and adds an AI-usage disclosure, and it now allows solo entries, cross-institution teams, and multiple teams per institution.

### Cited Findings
- 2026: 8 themes including "disaster mitigation"; teams up to 3 from the same institution; one team per institution; stages institutional -> state top 3 -> IIT Bombay screening (1 per State/UT, 36) -> national top 5; criteria: relevance, technical strength, prototype functionality, creativity, social impact, presentation, teamwork; submission: problem + beneficiary analysis, solution, tech stack/architecture, working prototype with open-source GitHub/GitLab link, 3–5 min demo video ≥720p; prizes Rs 75k/50k/25k/15k/15k; Rs 5,000 prototype grant per finalist — [contest360 summary](https://www.contest360.in/2025/12/hack-for-social-cause-2026-national-hackathon.html) (third-party)
- 2026 official page themes: Rural Development, Education & Skill Development, Healthcare, Urban Governance & Civic Tech, Environment & Sustainability, Digital Inclusion, AI GIS & Emerging Technologies; national online mentoring 19 Dec 2025–5 Jan 2026, offline mentoring 9–10 Jan, showcase and awards 11–12 Jan 2026 — [MY Bharat VBYLD 2026](https://mybharat.gov.in/pages/vbyld_2026)
- One team per college in 2026 — [Sadakath College circular](https://www.sadakath.ac.in/newscircular.php?id=13)
- 2027: 11 themes incl. "Disaster Mitigation, Kumbh Mela" / "Disaster Mitigation & Resilience"; up to 3 members, **no minimum (solo allowed)**, cross-institution allowed, multiple teams per institution allowed; encouraged: at least one female member and one non-tech (social sciences) member; 6–7 slide deck on IIT Bombay template with AI-usage disclosure; demo video 3–5 min (FAQ says preferred, not mandatory); public GitHub/GitLab with README, architecture docs, sample data, license; national 3-minute pitch at stalls; Rs 6,000 prototype grant for all 36 finalists; HSC 2026 finalists/winners barred; dates conflict on page (registration deadline 15 Oct vs 27 Oct 2026; state rounds 26 Oct–30 Nov vs 7 Nov–5 Dec 2026); national showcase 10–12 Jan 2027 — [MY Bharat HSC page](https://mybharat.gov.in/pages/hack-for-social-cause)

### Inferences
- The national round is a 3-minute stall pitch to a jury, so a physical or live demo that a juror can touch or see in seconds has an advantage (inference; supported by the 1st-place glove being a hands-on device shown to the PM).

### Gaps
- No published scoring weights for either year.

## Q6. Earlier editions and patterns in what won

### Takeaway
HSC 2026 (VBYLD 2026) appears to be the **first** edition; no earlier HSC edition was found. Patterns from 2026: a physical, demonstrable, user-tested prototype took 1st; solo entrants took 1st and 2nd; a J&K disaster-resilience project took 3rd.

### Cited Findings
- The 2027 rules refer only to "HSC 2026" finalists/winners as the prior edition — [MY Bharat HSC page](https://mybharat.gov.in/pages/hack-for-social-cause)
- Top 5 team sizes: 1, 1, 3, 3, 3 — [Winners PDF](https://cdn-wp.mybharats.in/designforBharat/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/19182319/National-Winner-HSC.pdf)
- 6 of 24 finalist teams were solo (Binnovators, Debugging Earth, Kisan AI Mitra, Dharika, Blue Light, SCIM TECHS) — [Finalists PDF](https://cdn-prod.mybharats.in/mybharat/assets/img/yuva_landing/HSC_National_Teams.pdf)
- Winner evidence of validation: demonstrated to doctors, doctor certificates, patent, cost (Rs 3,000) and plan to commercialise — [ETV Bharat](https://www.etvbharat.com/en/state/iiitdm-students-develop-special-gloves-for-paralysis-patients-receive-praise-from-pm-modi-enn26012206138)
- Winners came from non-elite institutions (a Haryana arts/commerce-type college, a private Roorkee institute, a government engineering college, a J&K state university) as well as IIITDM — [Winners PDF](https://cdn-wp.mybharats.in/designforBharat/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/19182319/National-Winner-HSC.pdf)

### Inferences
- **Solo entry is viable**: 2 of 5 winners were solo; solo is explicitly allowed in 2027 (inference + sourced rule).
- **Personal origin story plus real-user validation** seems to have mattered for 1st place (classmate with cerebral palsy; doctor sign-off) (inference from one case).
- **Hardware/physical demos** stood out at the stall-pitch format (inference; one data point).
- **Institution prestige was not decisive** (inference from winner list).
- Use of AI, GIS, government integration and local language among winners could **not** be verified; no project tech stacks were found except the glove (game-linked sensor device).
- For a solo J&K student in the Disaster theme, the bar is roughly: a working, demonstrable prototype with a public repo, evidence of testing with real stakeholders (e.g. SDRF/district officials, like the glove's doctor certificates), and a clear difference from 2026's A.N.T.S (inference).

### Gaps
- No VBYLD 2025 (or earlier National Youth Festival) HSC edition found; searches returned only 2026 and 2027 material. Not exhaustively ruled out.
- No YouTube or PIB coverage of the HSC award ceremony was found.

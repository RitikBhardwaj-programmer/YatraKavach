# Existing Indian disaster early-warning and crowd-management systems for pilgrimages and mountain routes, and the gaps left for YatraKavach (J&K)

Research date: 2026-10-23. Every bullet in "Cited Findings" is sourced. "Inferences" are labelled reasoning, not tested. Dates are given for every event. About 20 searches and fetches were made. Several official inquiry reports could not be found (see Gaps).

## 1. Amarnath Yatra (SASB): registration, RFID, apps, weather advisories, and the 8 July 2022 Holy Cave flood

### Takeaway
Amarnath has the most complete tech stack of any J&K yatra. It has mandatory online/bank registration with a health certificate, mandatory RFID cards, an official SASB app with SOS, live tracking and push notifications, and Doppler radars feeding an ICCC in Srinagar. But hazard alerts go mainly to "stakeholders" (camp commanders, the ICCC) and base-camp display boards, not to the individual pilgrim on a specific track segment. The 8 July 2022 flood happened under only a "yellow alert", in an area with no mountain-top weather stations.

### Cited Findings
- **2022 event:** On 8 July 2022 a flash flood near the Holy Cave killed at least 16 people and left more than 40 missing — [Wikipedia: 2022 Amarnath floods](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Amarnath_floods)
- IMD said the deaths came from a "highly localised rain event", not a cloudburst. The cave station recorded 31 mm between 16:30 and 18:30, below the cloudburst threshold of 100 mm/h — [Onmanorama, 9 Jul 2022](https://onmanorama.com/news/india/2022/07/09/amarnath-flash-floods-not-caused-by-cloudburst-imd.html)
- The daily forecast for the area on 8 July 2022 was a "yellow alert", and no other warning came with it — [Deccan Herald live blog, Jul 2022](https://www.deccanherald.com/india/india-weather-live-IMD-rains-flood-update-mumbai-karnataka-udupi-mangaluru-bengaluru-kodagu-delhi-kashmir-maharashtra-1124776.html)
- MoES told Rajya Sabha (28 Jul 2022) that the yatra was not allowed to start at 03:30 on 8 July because of IMD's rainfall forecast. It resumed at 05:00 on IMD advice. MoES also said the upper reaches may have had more rain, "for which data are not available" — [MoES Rajya Sabha answer 1300, 28 Jul 2022](https://www.moes.gov.in/sites/default/files/RS-in-English-1300-28-07-2022.pdf)
- IMD said the mountains around the cave have no weather monitoring stations because they are hard to reach — [Wikipedia summary citing IMD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Amarnath_floods)
- **Registration (2025):** Registration ran through jksasb.nic.in and about 540 authorised bank branches. It was mandatory and needed a Compulsory Health Certificate (CHC). The yatra ran 3 Jul–9 Aug 2025 on the Pahalgam and Baltal routes — [NewsOnAir, 2025](https://www.newsonair.gov.in/registrations-begin-for-52-day-amarnath-yatra-2025/); [Voice of Ladakh, Apr 2025](https://voiceofladakh.in/2025/04/registrations-begin-for-shri-amarnath-yatra-2025/)
- Sources conflict on the daily cap (about 15,000 vs 10,000 per day) and the fee (Rs 220 vs Rs 150) — [Drishti IAS summary, Mar 2025](https://www.drishtiias.com/statepcs/06-03-2025/) vs [India TV, 27 Jun 2025](https://www.indiatvnews.com/jammu-and-kashmir/amarnath-yatra-2025-solo-travel-banned-security-convoys-mandatory-key-rule-changes-for-pilgrims-2025-06-27-996503)
- **RFID (2025):** RFID cards were mandatory and issued at reception centres, with e-KYC at facilitation centres including the Nowgam and Katra railway stations. A Road Opening Party monitored every vehicle entering the yatra route. After the Pahalgam attack (Apr 2025), solo travel was banned and pilgrims had to move in security convoys — [India TV, 27 Jun 2025](https://www.indiatvnews.com/jammu-and-kashmir/amarnath-yatra-2025-solo-travel-banned-security-convoys-mandatory-key-rule-changes-for-pilgrims-2025-06-27-996503)
- **Official SASB app:** NeGD (MeitY) built it. Features include an SOS call alert, live tracking of yatris, push notifications and a weather forecast — [Kashmir Life](https://kashmirlife.net/mobile-app-developed-for-amarnath-yatra-213427/) (date not visible in the result)
- **Other apps:** The HUDD "Yatra Mitra" app (with Swaaha) gives helplines and maps of medical camps and lodging. ZeroWasteYatra.com gives "real-time weather and route updates" — [Greater Kashmir](https://greaterkashmir.com/jammu-kashmir/hudd-introduces-modern-technology-to-enhance-amarnath-yatra-experience). Anantnag Police run a QR-based "Pahchan" app to verify service providers (ponywalas, etc.) — [UNI](https://test.uniindia.com/anantnag-police-launch-pahchan-app-for-secure-amarnath-yatra/north/news/3866463.html)
- **Weather chain (2026 yatra, starting 3 Jul 2026):** Doppler Weather Radars at Srinagar, Banihal Top, Jammu and Leh cover the NH-44 and both routes, from base camps to the cave. An "integrated system" sends weather alerts and SMS to stakeholders (camp commanders, the ICCC Srinagar). Display boards at every base camp (Baltal, Nunwan) show real-time weather. NDMA has mapped the vulnerable spots on the route. 45 Mountain Rescue Teams are posted at 25 locations. Officials say the 2022 floods drove this upgrade. The article does not describe SMS reaching individual pilgrims — [WION, 26 Jun 2026](https://www.wionews.com/india-news/j-k-govt-strengthens-weather-surveillance-disaster-response-ahead-of-amarnath-yatra-1782472666513/amp)

### Inferences
- Amarnath already has registration, identity, RFID chokepoint tracking, an SOS app and radar nowcasting. YatraKavach must not rebuild these. It should consume them: RFID read counts give segment occupancy, and SASB registration gives a contact channel. (Inference.)
- The radar alerts stop at stakeholders and camp boards. A pilgrim already between Baltal and the cave, or at Panjtarni, gets no segment-specific push unless a camp official relays it. This last-mile, in-transit gap is the clearest one YatraKavach can fill. (Inference from the WION description.)
- The 2022 event fell below the formal cloudburst threshold and had no upstream gauges. Short-lead, very local rain over the cave's catchment is still poorly observed. (Inference.)

### Gaps
- I found no post-2022 official document that lists exactly what tech was added (beyond the radars, the MRTs and the boards). I did not check SASB's own site.
- I could not confirm whether the SASB app's push notifications have ever carried hazard warnings for a route segment, or how many pilgrims install it.

## 2. Vaishno Devi (SMVDSB): RFID yatra card, CCTV, and the 26 Aug 2025 Adhkuwari landslide

### Takeaway
SMVDSB has strong crowd-control tooling: mandatory RFID yatra cards, more than 700 CCTV cameras with real-time MIS, holding areas and a daily cap. But on 26 Aug 2025 a landslide on the old track near Adhkuwari killed about 34–35 pilgrims. The Board says it closed the old track at about 12:00, two hours before the 14:40 slide. Critics say it ignored "multiple" IMD alerts. The inquiry report has not been made public (as far as I could find). The crowd system knows where people are. It had no slope-hazard input.

### Cited Findings
- **RFID origin:** RFID was introduced after the stampede of 1 Jan 2022, which killed 12 pilgrims. Its aim is crowd management and tracking along the 13 km track and at Bhawan. LG Manoj Sinha (Board chairman) rolled out the system on 31 August (year not stated in the snippet; reports place it in 2022) — [The Statesman](https://www.thestatesman.com/india/lg-sinha-orders-introduction-rfid-tracking-crowd-management-vaishnodevi-1503035742.html); [Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/j-k/radio-frequency-identity-cards-in-offing-for-pilgrims-visiting-mata-vaishno-devi-shrine-in-jammu-and-kashmir-397085/amp)
- The cards are free and mandatory. No pilgrim may start without one. Flow is controlled to the holding capacity of each track section. There is e-surveillance through more than 700 CCTV cameras with "end-to-end MIS reporting on a real time basis". Four holding areas (Katra, Ardhkuwari, Sanjichhat, Manokamna) take the overflow above a 50,000/day limit — [Tribune: 700 cameras](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/j-k/vaishno-devi-shrine-sets-up-dedicated-network-of-over-700-cameras-for-e-surveillance-474077); [Daily Excelsior](https://www.dailyexcelsior.com/shrine-board-reviews-arrangements-ahead-of-chaitra-navratras/)
- **2025 event:** At 14:40 on 26 Aug 2025, a landslide hit the old track near Inderprastha Bhojanalya (Adhkuwari). At least 34 people died — [Tribune, 30 Aug 2025](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/j-k/no-lapses-vaishno-devi-board-calls-landslip-tragedy-unforeseeable). A later complaint cites 35 deaths — [Greater Kashmir](https://m.greaterkashmir.com/article/pilgrims-deaths-in-adhkuwari-landslide-incident-sub-judge-katra-seeks-atr-from-ssp-reasi-sho-bhawan/445157)
- **Board's timeline (self-reported):** The weather was clear until about 10:00. Fresh registrations were suspended after a "moderate rainfall" forecast (no time given). The old track was closed by 12:00 after a specific advisory. The newer Tarakote route had been closed since 24 Aug as a precaution. The Board called the slide force majeure, caused by a sudden cloudburst over a stretch of about 50 m, and denied negligence. No track-monitoring sensor technology is named, only staff and a Disaster Management Task Force — [Tribune, 30 Aug 2025](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/j-k/no-lapses-vaishno-devi-board-calls-landslip-tragedy-unforeseeable)
- **Criticism:** The Board was criticised for not suspending the pilgrimage despite "multiple" weather alerts. CM Omar Abdullah questioned why pilgrims were allowed to go on when heavy rain had been forecast — [Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/j-k/deadline-over-vaishno-devi-landslip-probe-report-not-out-yet); [Punjab Kesari/ANI, 30 Aug 2025](https://english.punjabkesari.com/india/2025/08/30/jammu-kashmir-landslides-block-path-vaishno-devi-yatra-suspended)
- **Inquiry:** On 29 Aug 2025 the LG set up a three-member committee (Shaleen Kabra, ACS Jal Shakti; Div Com Jammu Ramesh Kumar; IGP BS Tuti). It was to find causes and lapses and to propose SOPs within two weeks. The deadline passed with no public report. The inquiry into the Jan 2022 stampede was also never made public — [Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/j-k/deadline-over-vaishno-devi-landslip-probe-report-not-out-yet)
- A Katra sub-judge court asked the SSP Reasi and the SHO Bhawan for an Action Taken Report on a complaint that sought an FIR against the Board's CEO for criminal negligence — [Greater Kashmir](https://m.greaterkashmir.com/article/pilgrims-deaths-in-adhkuwari-landslide-incident-sub-judge-katra-seeks-atr-from-ssp-reasi-sho-bhawan/445157)
- The yatra resumed in mid-Sept 2025. Reports differ on the date: 14 Sept ([Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/j-k/mata-vaishno-devi-pilgrimage-to-resume-from-september-14)) vs 17 Sept (Punjab Kesari)

### Inferences
- The two accounts disagree on timing. The Board says it closed the track 2.5 h before the slide. Critics say it ignored alerts. Both may be partly true: the alerts were district- or region-level, and there was no rule that mapped an alert to "close segment X now, and clear the people already on it". (Inference.)
- RFID data shows how many pilgrims were between checkpoints, but there is no public evidence that it was used to evacuate the people already on the track after the noon closure. (Inference; not verified.)
- The decision trail (who received which alert, when, and what they did) is not public. This shows a need for logged, time-stamped acknowledgement of alerts. (Inference.)

### Gaps
- I found no public text of the Kabra committee report, and no result of the court matter (as of Oct 2026).
- I found no evidence of any slope sensor, rain gauge or piezometer on the Katra–Bhawan track.

## 3. Machail Mata yatra: Chasoti (Kishtwar) cloudburst of 14 Aug 2025

### Takeaway
IMD had issued cloudburst and flash-flood alerts for the Chenab valley. The yatra went on, and about 61–65 people died, mostly pilgrims, at Chasoti (the last motorable point). IMD's J&K director blamed "very little or negligible" compliance by people and administrations. After the event, MoES installed two EWS in Kishtwar/Machail and is installing an AWS in Padder (stated 1 Aug 2026).

### Cited Findings
- On 14 Aug 2025 a cloudburst hit Chasoti village, Paddar (Kishtwar). Tolls differ: 61 dead after four days ([Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/j-k/kishtwar-cloudburst-toll-61-search-for-trapped-victims-continues-on-day-4/amp)), 64 dead, mostly pilgrims ([ETV Bharat, 20 Aug 2025](https://www.etvbharat.com/en/!bharat/kishtwar-cloudburst-exposes-laxity-in-response-to-weather-warnings-in-jammu-kashmir-enn25082003499))
- IMD issued cloudburst and flash-flood alerts for the Chenab valley region. No level or time is given, and nothing shows a Kishtwar- or Chasoti-specific warning. The Machail yatra continued, which the article calls administrative "laxity" — [ETV Bharat, 20 Aug 2025](https://www.etvbharat.com/en/!bharat/kishtwar-cloudburst-exposes-laxity-in-response-to-weather-warnings-in-jammu-kashmir-enn25082003499)
- IMD J&K Director Mukhtar Ahmad said compliance with forecasts is "very little or negligible". He also said a response plan is needed in all 20 districts. The J&K DM department (created in 2016) still has no separate directorate. DMs and revenue officials lead the response and are overburdened. A DM Deputy Secretary said the response to flash floods and cloudbursts is weaker than to fires and floods because they happen so fast. IMD logged 2,863 extreme weather events and 552 deaths in J&K from 2010 to 2022 — [ETV Bharat](https://www.etvbharat.com/en/!bharat/kishtwar-cloudburst-exposes-laxity-in-response-to-weather-warnings-in-jammu-kashmir-enn25082003499)
- CM Omar Abdullah said the government would examine whether the administration could have taken preventive steps after the Met warning — [WION](https://embed.wionews.com/india-news/j-k-cloudburst-kills-at-least-60-in-kishtwar-s-chasoti-village-rescue-ops-underway-1755327357500)
- **After the event:** Union Minister Jitendra Singh said two new EWS were installed by MoES in Kishtwar and Machail, and an AWS was being installed in Padder (reported around 1 Aug 2026). A further Kishtwar cloudburst was reported around 2 Aug 2026 — [NewKerala, Aug 2026](https://www.newkerala.com/news/a/jk-cloudburst-agencies-deployed-clearing-operations-underway-kishtwar-548.htm) (search-result summary; details not fetched)

### Inferences
- The failure was not missing forecasts. It was the step from a region-level alert to a yatra-stop decision with a named owner, and onward to the pilgrims at Chasoti. (Inference.)
- Chasoti was a congested halt and langar point at the road-head. Crowd density at a known flash-flood channel multiplied the deaths. Crowd data plus hazard data at segment level is the missing combination. (Inference.)

### Gaps
- I found no published inquiry finding on the Chasoti warning chain.
- I could not find the type or vendor of the two "EWS" installed in Kishtwar/Machail (siren? rain gauge? AWS?).

## 4. Maha Kumbh 2025 (Prayagraj): ICCC, AI cameras and the crowd crush of 29 Jan 2025

### Takeaway
Kumbh 2025 had heavy AI/CCTV crowd analytics, but a pre-dawn crush on Mauni Amavasya (29 Jan 2025) killed 30 (official figure) to about 40 (Reuters sources). Anonymous officials say the cameras sent timely surge alerts, but too few police were on the ground to act. The lesson: detection without a linked, staffed response does not save lives.

### Cited Findings
- About 300 cameras on the site and the approach roads plus drones fed AI that tracked people flow and density at inlets, cross-checked with rail and bus data — [Context (Thomson Reuters Foundation)](https://www.context.news/ai/did-ai-fail-to-prevent-fatal-stampede-at-india-s-kumbh-festival); [WION, Jan 2025](https://www.wionews.com/india-news/maha-kumbh-2025-organisers-use-ai-to-prevent-stampedes-and-ensure-safety-of-400-million-pilgrims-8631920/amp)
- An unnamed official said the cameras sent timely surge alerts from every junction, but there were not enough police to prevent the jam. Another said a second crush began across the river with limited forces — [Context](https://www.context.news/ai/did-ai-fail-to-prevent-fatal-stampede-at-india-s-kumbh-festival)
- Police said 30 people died. Reuters sources said nearly 40. Witnesses gave different causes: a push toward the river, route closures that stopped the crowd, or barricades breaking — [Geo/Reuters](https://www.geo.tv/latest/588124-india-orders-probe-into-kumbh-festival-stampede-that-killed-dozens-mo13590792)
- A judicial commission (Justice Harsh Kumar, ex-DGP VK Gupta, DK Singh) was given one month to report. A PIL was also filed in the Supreme Court — [Scroll](https://scroll.in/latest/1078556/uttar-pradesh-government-orders-judicial-probe-into-maha-kumbh-stampede); [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/world/asia/india/india-top-court-to-hear-pil-demanding-action-over-maha-kumbh-stampede-1.500027371)

### Inferences
- For YatraKavach, alerts must create assigned and acknowledged tasks, with escalation if nobody acts. A dashboard alone is not enough. (Inference.)
- Kumbh is a flat, river-bank event with a fixed footprint. Mountain routes are linear, with single-file choke points and no escape routes, so the crowd and hazard models differ. (Inference.)

### Gaps
- I did not find the judicial commission's findings, or whether they were made public.

## 5. NDMA SACHET (CAP-based Integrated Alert System) and cell broadcast

### Takeaway
SACHET (C-DOT, CAP-based) is the national alert backbone. It runs in all states and UTs, in 19+ languages, and has sent billions of location-based SMS. Nationwide cell broadcast was tested on 2 May 2026 ahead of rollout. It targets areas (circles, districts, polygons), not identified pilgrims on a route segment. YatraKavach should publish CAP into SACHET, not compete with it.

### Cited Findings
- NDMA has put SACHET into operation (built by C-DOT), based on the ITU-recommended Common Alerting Protocol. It has sent "over 6,899 crore" SMS alerts in 19+ languages — [ETV Bharat, 2 May 2026](https://www.etvbharat.com/en/bharat/ndma-tests-cell-broadcast-message-to-alert-citizens-on-severe-disaster-enn26050201915). Another source says 134 billion — [Taxguru](https://taxguru.in/corporate-law/india-deploys-mobile-alert-system-due-rising-disaster-preparedness.html). The two figures conflict.
- Cell-broadcast testing began around 30 Jun 2025 — [IANS, 30 Jun 2025](https://ianslive.in/govt-begins-testing-new-mobile-alert-system-for-real-time-disaster-warnings--20250630200604). A nationwide test with a loud alarm tone ran on 2 May 2026, and HM Amit Shah launched the "Cell Broadcast Alert System" — [IANS, 2 May 2026](https://ianslive.in/govt-launches-cell-broadcast-alert-system-for-real-time-disaster-warnings--20260502131531)
- Tests reached only handsets with Cell Broadcast test channels enabled. Full nationwide operation was to follow — [ETV Bharat, 2 May 2026](https://www.etvbharat.com/en/bharat/ndma-tests-cell-broadcast-message-to-alert-citizens-on-severe-disaster-enn26050201915)

### Inferences
- Cell broadcast needs working cell coverage. Large parts of the Amarnath, Machail and Paddar tracks have weak or no coverage, so SACHET alone cannot reach pilgrims in those dead zones. (Inference; coverage not measured.)
- SACHET has no record of who acknowledged or acted on an alert at the administrative level. YatraKavach could add an approval and audit layer whose output is a CAP message. (Inference.)

### Gaps
- I did not confirm the current (Oct 2026) operational status of cell broadcast in J&K specifically, or the Sachet app's features and install base.

## 6. Landslide EWS in India (Amrita, IIT Mandi, GSI, Uttarakhand, Sikkim GLOF) and J&K's own initiatives

### Takeaway
India's landslide EWS work is either (a) dense sensor networks at a few sites (Amrita Munnar since about 2009; Chandmari, Gangtok; IIT Mandi in Mandi district), or (b) district-level rainfall-threshold bulletins (GSI's Bhusanket: 21 districts in 8 states in 2025, none in J&K). J&K relies on IMD radars (3–4 now; 4 more planned) and a new AWS and snow-gauge push. No route-segment landslide warning exists for any J&K yatra track (as far as I could find).

### Cited Findings
- **Amrita:** Amrita says it built the world's first wireless sensor network for landslide EWS at Munnar (Kerala), working since 2009. It issued a verified warning on 5 Jul 2023 that was emailed to the state government — [Amrita](https://www.amrita.edu/?p=449714). An EGU26 abstract says warnings go back to 2005, which conflicts — [EGU26-20814](https://meetingorganizer.copernicus.org/EGU26/EGU26-20814.html). Sikkim: more than 200 sensors over about 150 acres at Chandmari (Gangtok), costing Rs 5 crore (SSDMA + MoES) — [Assam Tribune](https://assamtribune.com/sikkim-gets-real-time-landslide-warning-system). Amrita reports 9 warnings from the Sikkim/Darjeeling system (self-reported)
- **IIT Mandi:** A real-time monitoring EWS runs at three sites in Mandi district. The team claims an ML model with more than 90% accuracy — [Careers360](https://news.careers360.com/iit-mandi-scientists-develop-landslide-warning-system-capable-of-detecting-minute-shifts-in-ground). An operational system also gives daily forecasts for the Indian Himalayan Region through a web portal and WhatsApp, using landslide inventories and satellite rainfall — [Ground News](https://ground.news/article/iit-mandi-develops-operational-landslide-early-warning-system-for-indian-himalayan-region)
- **GSI:** For the 2025 monsoon, GSI issued operational and experimental landslide forecast bulletins for 21 districts in 8 states. Operational: Darjeeling, Kalimpong, Nilgiris. Experimental: 6 Sikkim districts, Wayanad, Idukki, Kodagu and others. Rudraprayag (Uttarakhand) operational bulletins were "coming soon". The bulletins are colour-coded per district on the Bhusanket portal and the Bhooskhalan app. GSI plans AI models — [MoES/PIB](https://moes.gov.in/sites/default/files/PIB2158420.pdf); [Business Standard, 20 Jul 2025](https://www.business-standard.com/india-news/gsi-plans-to-use-ai-to-develop-robust-landslide-forecasting-model-dg-125072000196_1.html); [NIDM GSI deck](https://nidm.gov.in/pdf/acrc26/Preparatory%20Session/3.%20GSI.pdf)
- **Uttarakhand:** USDMA's "U-Prepare" multi-hazard EWS (landslides, floods, earthquakes) was announced around Dec 2024. Agencies are paid only for accurate, timely information — [Swarajya](https://swarajyamag.com/amp/story/news-brief/uttarakhand-prepares-early-warning-system-for-natural-hazards-to-prevent-loss-of-life-and-propertyheres-all-about-u-scheme). IIT Roorkee has a rainfall-based model with at least 6 h lead time ([Drishti](https://drishtiias.com/pdf/1717524097.pdf)). Char Dham Yatra is suspended in heavy rain through blanket administrative orders — [Free Press Journal](https://www.freepressjournal.in/amp/lifestyle/uttarakhands-char-dham-yatra-suspended-as-heavy-rains-trigger-landslide-concerns-along-pilgrimage-routes)
- **Sikkim GLOF:** There was no EWS when South Lhonak burst (4 Oct 2023). Two AWS had been installed on 16 Sep 2023, and a tripwire sensor was planned but not complete — [Down To Earth](https://www.downtoearth.org.in/natural-disasters/sikkim-floods-why-wasn-t-early-warning-system-set-up-at-glacial-lake-known-to-be-highly-vulnerable-experts-ask-92116); [Mongabay, Oct 2023](https://india.mongabay.com/2023/10/no-early-warning-system-and-insufficient-dam-safety-turned-sikkim-flood-deadly/). Swiss-supported monitoring stations at South Lhonak and Shako Cho are now used daily by SSDMA/NDMA, and they feed "future" EWS — [Geopraevent](https://www.geopraevent.ch/project/glof-monitoring-systems-in-sikkim-india/?lang=en)
- **J&K:** 3 DWRs operate (Srinagar, Jammu, Banihal; plus Leh in Ladakh). 4 more are planned (Rajouri, Doda, Anantnag, Sopore), which would make 7. The CM says cloudbursts are still hard to predict — [NewsOnAir, 7 Aug 2026](https://newsonair.gov.in/imd-to-install-4-additional-doppler-weather-radars-in-jk/). Plans include 26 more AWS and 8 snow gauges (Kishtwar, Doda, Ramban, Rajouri) and an IMD hyper-local cloudburst forecasting system across the Himalayan states. Ramban and Kishtwar are the priority districts. Anantnag, Kulgam, Ganderbal, the Chenab valley, Reasi and Udhampur are rated "high vulnerable" — [Greater Kashmir (date unclear, ~Feb 2026)](https://m.greaterkashmir.com/article/cloud-bursts-landslides-flash-floods-anantnag-kulgam-ganderbal-chenab-valley-reasi-udhampur-inhigh-vulnerable-category-cm-omar-abdullah/480569)

### Inferences
- No J&K district appears in GSI's 2025 landslide-bulletin list. Reasi (Vaishno Devi), Kishtwar (Machail) and Anantnag/Ganderbal (Amarnath) seem to have no operational regional landslide forecast. (Inference from the published list. Verify on bhusanket.gsi.gov.in.)
- Point sensor networks (Amrita type) cost about Rs 5 crore per 150 acres. That is too costly for a full 13–48 km track, so a route system would mix a few sensors at known hotspots with rainfall-threshold logic per segment. (Estimate: the cost scaling is my own reasoning.)

### Gaps
- I found no source on a "J&K DMRRR" EWS programme. I also could not confirm the World Bank-funded Uttarakhand Disaster Recovery Project's EWS parts or any Char Dham route sensors.

## 7. Google Flood Hub and Google alerts in India

### Takeaway
Google's ML flood forecasting started in Patna (2018) and covers India's major riverine flood areas. It delivers alerts through Search, Maps and Android notifications. It is riverine and does not cover flash floods or landslides on mountain tracks (no Google landslide product for India was found).

### Cited Findings
- Started in Patna in 2018. Later it covered "all the worst flood-hit areas of India", reaching about 200M people in India, with more than 30M Android notifications sent. It uses rainfall, river levels and flood simulations, not phone data — [Google blog](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/expanding-our-ml-based-flood-forecasting/); [TBS News](https://www.tbsnews.net/node/127144)
- Forecasts appear in Google Search, Maps and Android notifications — [ADN40, 2 Feb 2026](https://www.adn40.mx/enlace-latino/2026-02-02/google-floods-el-mapa-que-te-alerta-inundaciones-antes-salir/)

### Inferences
- Flood Hub's gauged-river model does not fit cloudburst-driven nala (small stream) floods on yatra tracks such as Chasoti or near the cave. YatraKavach could use its forecasts as an outside input for valley-floor segments (e.g. Lidder, Chenab). (Inference.)

### Gaps
- I found no evidence of a Google landslide alert product in India. Flood Hub coverage for J&K rivers was not checked.

## 9. Summary of gaps YatraKavach could fill (without duplicating)

### Takeaway
Existing systems split into silos. Hazard forecasting (IMD radar, GSI district bulletins, Google rivers) works at district or region scale. Crowd systems (RFID, CCTV, ICCC) know where people are, but have no hazard input. Mass alerting (SACHET, cell broadcast) targets areas, not people in transit. Decision accountability is undocumented. The three 2025 deaths-in-yatra events (Chasoti on 14 Aug, Adhkuwari on 26 Aug) and the 2022 Amarnath flood all show the same break: the warning existed in some form, but it did not become a timely, owned, segment-level stop-and-clear action that reached the pilgrims.

### Cited Findings
- District-level only: GSI bulletins are per district, and IMD alerts were for the "Chenab valley region" — [MoES/PIB](https://moes.gov.in/sites/default/files/PIB2158420.pdf); [ETV Bharat](https://www.etvbharat.com/en/!bharat/kishtwar-cloudburst-exposes-laxity-in-response-to-weather-warnings-in-jammu-kashmir-enn25082003499)
- Alerts stop at stakeholders and camp boards — [WION, 26 Jun 2026](https://www.wionews.com/india-news/j-k-govt-strengthens-weather-surveillance-disaster-response-ahead-of-amarnath-yatra-1782472666513/amp)
- Response was weak even when alerts existed: "negligible" compliance, and AI alerts with too few police — [ETV Bharat](https://www.etvbharat.com/en/!bharat/kishtwar-cloudburst-exposes-laxity-in-response-to-weather-warnings-in-jammu-kashmir-enn25082003499); [Context](https://www.context.news/ai/did-ai-fail-to-prevent-fatal-stampede-at-india-s-kumbh-festival)
- Opaque accountability: inquiry reports were not published — [Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/j-k/deadline-over-vaishno-devi-landslip-probe-report-not-out-yet)
- Sparse observations in the upper catchments — [MoES RS 1300](https://www.moes.gov.in/sites/default/files/RS-in-English-1300-28-07-2022.pdf)

### Inferences (candidate YatraKavach niches, all inference)
1. **Route-segment risk:** Split each track into segments (e.g. Baltal–Domail–Brarimarg–Sangam–Cave; Katra–Banganga–Adhkuwari–Sanjichhat–Bhawan; Gulabgarh–Chasoti–Machail). Score each segment from IMD radar or nowcast, rain thresholds, slope and channel exposure, and GSI susceptibility maps.
2. **Crowd × hazard fusion:** Use the existing RFID checkpoint counts (SASB, SMVDSB) to estimate how many people are on each at-risk segment *now*. That number sets priority for holds, reversals and clearing. Neither Board shows this in public.
3. **Human-approved, audited alerts:** Recommend actions (hold at gate, stop ascent, clear segment), require a named official to approve them, time-stamp the acknowledgements, and escalate if nobody acts. This answers the "who decided" gap in the Vaishno Devi and Chasoti cases.
4. **Last-mile in transit:** Send output as CAP to SACHET and cell broadcast, plus SASB app push, SMS to registered mobiles, PA/siren at camps, and ponywala and police radio relay for no-signal stretches.
5. **Language:** Hindi, Dogri, Kashmiri, Punjabi, Gujarati, Bengali and others for pan-India pilgrims (SACHET supports 19+ languages; per-pilgrim language choice comes from registration data).
6. **Smaller yatras:** Machail and similar yatras without RFID or apps could get light registration plus QR checkpoint counts.
7. **Do NOT duplicate:** registration, RFID hardware, CCTV analytics, the SASB SOS app, IMD radar or forecasting, GSI district bulletins, or SACHET distribution. YatraKavach should integrate with these.

### Gaps
- Mobile coverage maps along the yatra tracks, the APIs exposed by SASB/SMVDSB RFID systems, and IMD nowcast data access terms were not researched.
